## Description
InkReader is a quiet, comfortable reader for PDFs and e-books, built for people who read for hours.

Most PDF readers show thin gray text on glaring white, scroll and animate constantly, and surround the page with toolbars. InkReader does the opposite. It shows dark, sharp text on soft warm paper, turns whole pages at a time, and lets the page fill your screen with nothing else around it.

It was designed for e-ink monitors (Dasung Paperlike, Boox Mira and e-ink tablets used as a second screen), and it's just as easy on the eyes on an ordinary laptop or desktop display.

EASY ON THE EYES
• Soft paper styles: Warm paper, Soft white, Sepia, Low-glare gray, Pure white and Night (white on black)
• Paper brightness control for late-night reading
• Dark app frame, so the page is the only bright thing on screen

DISTRACTION-FREE READING MODE (F11)
• Full screen with just the page, filling the whole width of your screen
• The pointer hides while you read, and the toolbar appears only when you move to the top edge
• Remembers its own layout, such as two pages side by side on a wide monitor

SHARP, COMFORTABLE TEXT
• ClearType-style subpixel text rendering for crisp letters on LCD screens
• 2× to 3× high-resolution rendering, with darker and bolder text options
• Automatic margin cropping, so the text is as large as possible

BUILT FOR E-INK MONITORS
• Whole-page turns, with no smooth scrolling or animation
• 16-gray, black & white and dithered rendering modes
• One-key screen refresh (F5) to clear ghosting

EVERYTHING YOU NEED
• Fit page, fit width, two pages and zoom
• Search, table of contents, clickable links, go to page and rotate
• Opens PDF, EPUB, MOBI, FB2, XPS and CBZ; e-books re-flow, with adjustable font size
• Remembers the page you were on in every document
• Keyboard shortcuts for everything, plus large click zones for turning pages

PRIVATE AND OPEN
InkReader runs entirely on your computer. It has no accounts, no tracking and no ads, and it uses no network connection of its own. It's free and open source under the GNU AGPL v3, and the source code is on GitHub.
