## Description
InkReader is a quiet, comfortable reader for PDFs and e-books, built for people who read for hours.

Most PDF readers show thin gray text on glaring white and surround the page with toolbars. InkReader does the opposite. It shows dark, sharp text on soft warm paper, moves calmly through your document, and lets the page fill your screen with nothing else around it.

It was designed for e-ink monitors (Dasung Paperlike, Boox Mira and e-ink tablets used as a second screen), and it's just as easy on the eyes on an ordinary laptop or desktop display.

EASY ON THE EYES
• Soft paper styles: Warm paper, Soft white, Sepia, Low-glare gray, Pure white and Night (white on black)
• Paper brightness control for late-night reading
• Dark app frame, so the page is the only bright thing on screen

DISTRACTION-FREE READING MODE (F11)
• Full screen with just the page, filling the whole width of your screen
• The pointer hides while you read, and the toolbar appears only when you move to the top edge
• Remembers its own layout, such as two pages side by side on a wide monitor

SMOOTH SCROLLING THAT KEEPS YOUR PLACE
• The mouse wheel and arrow keys glide a few lines; Space and PgDn glide a whole screen
• After each screen turn, an orange mark highlights the last line you were reading, so your eyes find your place instantly
• On e-ink panels, scrolling stays as instant page turns (animation smears on e-ink)

CLICKABLE WORDS AND SENTENCES
• Click a word, double-click to select its whole sentence, or drag across any phrase
• Copy the text, find it in the document, or look up a word's definition
• Explain: get a plain-language explanation of the selection from Google AI Mode

FULL COLOR WHEN YOU NEED IT
• One click (or K) switches to true colors for photos, charts and colored text
• Colors sit on your chosen paper; on the Night paper they keep their hue
• Press K again to return to your gray or e-ink mode

SHARP, COMFORTABLE TEXT
• ClearType-style subpixel text rendering for crisp letters on LCD screens
• 2× to 3× high-resolution rendering, with darker and bolder text options
• Automatic margin cropping, so the text is as large as possible

BUILT FOR E-INK MONITORS
• Whole-page turns with no animation in the e-ink modes
• 16-gray, black & white and dithered rendering modes
• One-key screen refresh (F5) to clear ghosting

EVERYTHING YOU NEED
• Fit page, fit width, two pages and zoom
• Search, table of contents, clickable links, go to page and rotate
• Opens PDF, EPUB, MOBI, FB2, XPS and CBZ; e-books re-flow, with adjustable font size
• Remembers the page you were on in every document
• Keyboard shortcuts for everything

PRIVATE AND OPEN
InkReader runs entirely on your computer. It has no accounts, no tracking and no ads, and it uses no network connection of its own. Define and Explain open your web browser only when you click them, sending just the text you selected. It's free and open source under the GNU AGPL v3, and the source code is on GitHub.

## What's new in this version (1.8.0)
• Full color: press K or click the new Color button to see photos, charts and colored text in their true colors, on your chosen paper. On the Night paper, colors keep their hue and photos stay natural.
• Explain with Google AI Mode: double-click a word to select its whole sentence, then press Explain to get a plain-language explanation in your browser.
• The word bar now offers Copy, Find, Explain and Define.
• Rotate and Refresh moved to the right-click menu (keys R and F5) so the toolbar fits smaller windows.
