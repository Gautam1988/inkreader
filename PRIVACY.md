# InkReader privacy policy

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

InkReader is a document reader that runs entirely on your computer. **It does not collect, store, send or share any personal information.**

## What stays on your computer

InkReader saves a few things locally so it can remember where you were:

- your display settings (paper style, zoom, rendering options)
- the list of recently opened documents and the page you were on in each
- a small diagnostic log of start-ups, exits and errors

These are kept in `%APPDATA%\InkReader` (for the Microsoft Store version, in the app's private storage). They never leave your computer. To delete them, uninstall InkReader or delete that folder.

The documents you open are read from your disk and are never uploaded anywhere.

## No network access

InkReader has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own. It opens your web browser only when you click a link yourself:

- a web link inside a document (InkReader asks before opening it)
- **Source on GitHub** or **Source code**, which open the project page on github.com. GitHub's own privacy statement applies there.

## Children

InkReader collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released.
